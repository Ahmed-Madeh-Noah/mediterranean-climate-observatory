    1 · Parametric + Memory

    OLS with Newey–West HAC

    Climate time series rarely consist of independent yearly draws—warm ocean anomalies persist across consecutive years. Newey–West standard errors inflate uncertainty using Bartlett-weighted residual autocovariances so serial correlation does not produce false significance.

    2 · Distribution-Free

    Mann–Kendall & Theil–Sen

    Coastal precipitation is heavily skewed by rare storm years. Theil–Sen takes the median of all pairwise slopes between years, resisting single-year outliers, while Mann–Kendall tests monotonic rank order without assuming Gaussian residuals.

    3 · Multiplicity Control

    Benjamini–Hochberg FDR

    Testing multiple variables or multiple cities simultaneously increases the chance of a false positive at α = 0.05. Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted q-values control the expected false discovery rate across each hypothesis family.

    Scientific Integrity & Limitations

    Methodological guardrails for interpreting trend claims

    Responsible Earth system trend detection requires stating what a dataset can and cannot support before drawing policy or ecological conclusions.

    Guardrail 1

    Short windows manufacture false trends

    Over 10–15 year slices, natural decadal oscillations (such as the Atlantic Multidecadal Oscillation or local storm clustering) easily masquerade as climate trends or mask real ones. Always inspect sliding-window stability before trusting a short record.

    Guardrail 2

    Autocorrelation inflates plain t-tests

    Upper-ocean heat content and surface salinity have strong multi-year memory (r₁ > 0.6). A standard OLS t-test treats 76 correlated years as 76 independent samples, understating p-values by orders of magnitude.

    Guardrail 3

    Basin means vs. single 0.1° grid cells

    Basin-averaged SST, sea level, and chlorophyll smooth out local weather noise over ~2.5 million km². By contrast, GPM IMERG rainfall series represent single ~10 km × 10 km coastal land pixels where one convective storm can double the annual total.

    Guardrail 4

    Correlation is not causal attribution

    Observing concurrent warming, sea-level rise, and declining surface chlorophyll is physically consistent with thermal expansion and stronger water-column stratification, but statistical trend tests alone do not partition anthropogenic vs. internal forcing.
